WHO WE ARE

About TJ Foundation

TJ Foundation is dedicated to the welfare of all animals. We aim to rescue, treat, shelter and protect animals in need, regardless of their species. Our mission is to create a kinder, safer and more compassionate world for every living being.

♥ Our Mission

To rescue, treat and protect all animals in need and give them a better tomorrow.

◉ Our Vision

A society where every animal is treated with kindness, respect and care.

🐾 Our Values

Compassion, responsibility, integrity and respect for all living beings.

WHAT WE DO

Our Work

From the first rescue call to full recovery, we stay with the animal at every step.

🚑

Emergency Rescue

Responding to distress calls for injured, sick or trapped animals and getting them to safety quickly.

🩺

Medical Treatment

Coordinating veterinary care, first aid, sterilisation and vaccination for animals who need it most.

🏠

Shelter & Rehabilitation

Providing a safe place to rest, heal and recover before animals are reunited or rehomed.

🍲

Feeding Drives

Regular feeding of community strays and animals in areas where food and water are scarce.

📢

Awareness & Education

Working with communities and schools to spread kindness, empathy and responsible animal care.

🤝

Adoption Support

Helping rescued animals find loving, responsible homes through our adoption network.
